About

Independent by design.

AntiquePepe is an independent game development brand focused on building high-quality games for mobile and PC.

The goal is simple: games that are easy to pick up, rewarding to master and carefully crafted down to the details. From refined takes on classic strategy games to larger PC projects, every title is developed with attention to clean design, fair play and a polished experience.

Mobile

Focused games for iOS and Android, designed for short sessions and long-term depth.

PC

Larger, more ambitious projects built for desktop players.

Craft

Minimal interfaces, thoughtful mechanics and attention to detail.
