Writing a helpful bug report

The more details you include, the faster we can find and fix the issue:

  1. Game – e.g. Gomoku: Ascension, plus the app version if you know it.
  2. Device and system – e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18 or Pixel 8, Android 15.
  3. What happened – and what you expected to happen instead.
  4. Steps to reproduce – what you did right before the problem occurred.
  5. Screenshots or screen recordings – if possible.

Please don't send passwords or other sensitive personal data. Information you send by e-mail is used only to handle your request — see our Privacy Policy.
